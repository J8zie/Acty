About Actify

From clogged drains to dark streets, every small problem has someone nearby willing to help.

Actify is a community reporting platform for Tagum City. Residents pin what is broken in their barangay, volunteers nearby pick it up, and anyone can follow a report from open to resolved.

Non-emergency reports only. In an emergency, call 911.
